About East Chem

We started East Chem to close the gap between what's on the agro-shop shelf and what farmers actually know how to do with it.

Our mission

Supply genuine, well-matched agricultural inputs — and make sure the person buying them understands exactly how to use them safely and effectively.

How we got here

Founded

East Chem PLC registered to serve agro-dealers and cooperatives with fertilizer and crop-protection supply.

Expanded reach

Grew distribution beyond regional capitals into woreda-level agro-dealers.

Added guidance

Began pairing every product line with plain-language usage and safety instructions.

Today

Serving farmers and distributors with a growing product catalog and multilingual support.
